Below is a **complete brand identity document** for **Quitra**, designed to
position it as a **modern, premium, behavior-change product**—not just another
habit tracker.

---

# 🧾 Quitra — Brand Identity Document

---

# 1. 🧠 Brand Core

## Brand Name:

**Quitra**

## Name Rationale:

- Derived from _“Quit”_ with a soft, modern suffix
- Feels like a **product**, not a feature
- Easy to pronounce globally
- Distinct enough for App Store + branding

---

## Brand Mission:

Help people **break free from smoking** through intelligent support and
behavioral design.

---

## Brand Vision:

To become the **most trusted digital companion** for quitting harmful habits.

---

## Brand Values:

- **Control** → You are in charge, not the addiction
- **Clarity** → Simple, focused experience
- **Compassion** → No guilt, no punishment
- **Progress** → Every step matters
- **Privacy** → Your journey is yours

---

# 2. 🎯 Brand Positioning

## Market Position:

> A **premium, psychology-driven quit companion**

NOT:

- ❌ a basic tracker
- ❌ a medical app
- ❌ a lecture-based coach

---

## Value Proposition:

> “Quitra helps you stay in control at the moments that matter most.”

---

## Differentiation:

- Real-time craving support
- Emotional UX (not cold statistics)
- Minimal, non-judgmental design
- Offline-first privacy

---

# 3. 🗣️ Brand Voice & Tone

---

## Tone Attributes:

- Calm
- Supportive
- Confident
- Non-judgmental
- Direct

---

## Writing Style:

### ✅ Good:

- “You’re doing better than you think.”
- “Cravings pass. This will too.”
- “One step at a time.”

### ❌ Avoid:

- “You must quit now.”
- “Smoking is bad.”
- “Failure is not an option.”

---

## Messaging Principle:

> Speak like a **trusted companion**, not a strict coach.

---

# 4. 🎨 Visual Identity

---

## 4.1 Color Palette

### Primary Color:

- **Deep Teal**

  - HEX: `#0F766E`
  - Meaning: calm, control, clarity

### Secondary Colors:

- Soft Green → `#22C55E` (progress, success)
- Light Gray → `#F3F4F6` (clean UI)
- Dark Gray → `#111827` (text, contrast)

### Accent:

- Warm Orange → `#F59E0B`

  - Used for:

    - cravings
    - alerts

---

## Color Strategy:

- Calm base (teal)
- Positive reinforcement (green)
- Urgency only when needed (orange)

---

## 4.2 Typography

### Primary Font:

- **Inter** (Flutter-friendly, clean)

### Usage:

- Headlines → SemiBold
- Body → Regular
- Stats → Bold

---

## 4.3 Logo Concept

### Style:

- Minimal
- Rounded edges
- Modern

### Symbol Ideas:

- “Q” shaped like a **broken circle** (breaking habit)
- Subtle **breath wave / airflow**
- Path/line showing progress

---

## Logo Feel:

- Clean
- Trustworthy
- Not medical, not playful

---

# 5. 📱 UI Design Principles

---

## Core Principles:

### 1. Minimal Cognitive Load

- No clutter
- One primary action per screen

---

### 2. Emotional Feedback

- Celebrate small wins
- Reduce guilt on relapse

---

### 3. Speed First

- Open → act within 2 seconds
- Especially for craving moments

---

### 4. Focus on “Now”

- Not just long-term stats
- Immediate help

---

# 6. 🧩 UX Personality

---

## Quitra feels like:

- A calm friend sitting next to you
- Not a doctor
- Not a strict coach
- Not a gamified toy

---

## During craving:

- Fast
- Grounding
- Reassuring

---

## During success:

- Subtle celebration
- Not overwhelming

---

# 7. 📦 Feature Naming (Important for Branding)

Avoid technical names.

---

## Instead of:

- “Daily Logs” → ✅ “Your Day”
- “Craving Tracker” → ✅ “Moments”
- “Statistics” → ✅ “Your Progress”
- “Relapse” → ✅ “Setback”

---

# 8. 🧠 Emotional Design System

---

## States & Messaging:

### 🟢 Success:

- “You made it through today.”

### 🟡 Craving:

- “Pause. Breathe. This will pass.”

### 🔴 Setback:

- “It’s okay. You’re still moving forward.”

# 10. 🧲 Growth Identity

---

## Shareable Identity:

Users should share:

- “Day 7 smoke-free”
- “Saved $50”
- “I didn’t give in today”

---

## Design:

- Clean stat cards
- Dark/light versions
- Minimal text, strong visuals

---

# 11. ⚠️ Brand Mistakes to Avoid

---

- ❌ Too medical (feels like hospital app)
- ❌ Too playful (loses seriousness)
- ❌ Too aggressive (pushes users away)
- ❌ Too complex UI

---

# 12. 🧭 Future Brand Expansion

---

Quitra can later expand to:

- vaping
- sugar addiction
- phone addiction

So keep branding **broad, not cigarette-specific**

---

# 🔚 Final Positioning Statement

> Quitra is a calm, intelligent companion that helps people take back
> control—one decision at a time.
