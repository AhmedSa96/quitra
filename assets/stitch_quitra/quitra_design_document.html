# 📱 Quitra — UI/UX Design Document ## 1. 🧠 UX Philosophy - **Core Principle:** Most useful at the moment of craving. - **Goals:** < 2s to action, zero friction, emotionally supportive, minimal cognitive load. ## 2. 🗺️ App Structure - **Bottom Nav:** Home, Progress, Journey, Settings. ## 3. 🧩 Screen Designs ### 3.1 Home Screen - Greeting: "Day 5 smoke-free" - Primary CTA: Large "I feel like smoking" craving button. - Quick stats: Money saved, Cigarettes avoided, Time smoke-free. - Daily check-in card. ### 3.2 Craving Flow - Trigger: From Home button. - Step 1: "Pause. Breathe." with animation. - Step 2: Options like "30s reset", "Distract me", "Remind me why". - Step 3: Positive reinforcement (stats). ### 3.3 Daily Check-in (Modal) - Inputs: Did you smoke? (Toggle), Craving level (Slider 1-5), Optional note. ### 3.4 Progress Screen - Key Metrics cards. - Charts: Weekly trend, Streak visualization. - Health Milestones cards. ### 3.5 Journey Screen - Timeline list of daily status (clean, craving, smoked). ## 4. 🎨 Design System - **Colors:** Primary (#0F766E), Success (#22C55E), Warning (#F59E0B), Background (#F3F4F6), Text (#111827). - **Components:** Rounded buttons (16-24 radius), Cards with soft shadow and 16 radius. - **Grid:** 8pt. ## 7. 🚀 Onboarding - Steps: Cigarettes/day, Years smoking, Quit method, Start date. ## 8. ⚠️ Critical UX - Craving button must always be visible. - Speed > beauty. - No feature overload.
