Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Overview

Quitra ("the App") is a strictly offline smoking cessation companion. We do not collect, store, transmit, or process any personal data on external servers. Your journey is yours — completely.

Data We Collect

None. Quitra does not collect any data from you. All information you enter into the app — including your quit date, smoking habits, craving logs, and health milestones — is stored exclusively on your device using a local database (Isar).

No Servers, No Analytics, No Tracking

Quitra does not:

  • Connect to any external server
  • Use analytics or tracking SDKs
  • Send crash reports or usage data
  • Use advertising identifiers
  • Share data with third parties

Data Portability

You can export all your data as CSV files at any time using the in-app export feature. This allows you to back up your data manually or transfer it to another device. The exported files remain under your full control.

Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it at any time by:

  • Using the in-app data deletion feature in Settings
  • Uninstalling the app (this permanently removes all app data)

For more information, see our Data Deletion page.

Children's Privacy

Quitra is not directed at children under 13. Since we do not collect any data, there is no risk of processing children's information.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
