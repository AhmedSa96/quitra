Data Deletion

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Your Data, Your Control

Quitra stores all data exclusively on your device. We have no access to your data — it never leaves your phone. This means you have complete control over your data at all times.

How to Delete Your Data

Uninstalling Quitra from your device will permanently delete all data associated with the app. This action cannot be undone.

Before You Delete

We recommend exporting your data as a backup before deletion. Use the CSV Export feature in Settings to save a copy of your records to your device's file system.

No Server-Side Data

Since Quitra is 100% offline with no external servers, there is no server-side data to delete. Uninstalling the app is a complete and permanent removal of all information.
